The Speakers

Both scholars serve with Reasons to Believe, an organization demonstrating the compatibility of science and the Christian faith.

Dr. Hugh Ross

Astrophysicist · Founder, Reasons to Believe

Founder and senior scholar of Reasons to Believe. Hugh earned a degree in physics from the University of British Columbia and a PhD in astronomy from the University of Toronto, then researched quasars and galaxies as a postdoctoral fellow at Caltech before moving into full-time ministry.

Dr. Fazale “Fuz” Rana

Biochemist · President & CEO, Reasons to Believe

Fuz attended West Virginia State College and earned a PhD in chemistry from Ohio University, with postdoctoral work at the Universities of Virginia and Georgia. He now serves as President and CEO of Reasons to Believe.
